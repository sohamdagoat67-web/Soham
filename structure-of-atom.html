⚛️ Atom Details

An atom is the basic unit of an element. It contains a tiny central nucleus surrounded by electrons.

🟣 Nucleus
Contains protons and neutrons.
➕ Proton
Has a positive electric charge.
⚪ Neutron
Has no electric charge.
🔵 Electron
Has a negative electric charge.

💡 Did You Know?

An atom is mostly empty space. The nucleus is incredibly tiny compared with the size of the whole atom.
