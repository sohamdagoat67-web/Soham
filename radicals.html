🧪 CORE CONCEPT

What is a Radical?

A radical is a group of atoms that carries a charge and behaves as a single unit in a chemical compound.

⚡ Think of a radical as a group of atoms travelling together with one overall charge.
CO₃²⁻ Carbonate
PO₄³⁻ Phosphate
SO₄²⁻ Sulphate
NO₃⁻ Nitrate
