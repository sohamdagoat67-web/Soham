Molecules and Atomicity

The 5 Types of Atomicity

  • Monoatomic: Contains only one atom
  • Diatomic: Contains two atoms
  • Triatomic: Contains three atoms
  • Tetratomic: Contains four atoms
  • Polyatomic: Contains more than four atoms
Type of Atomicity Number of Atoms Elements Molecular Formula

Diatomic 2 Hydrogen, Nitrogen, Oxygen, Iodine, Fluorine, Chlorine, Bromine H2, N2, O2, I2, F2, Cl2, Br2
Triatomic 3 Ozone O3

Polyatomic More than 4 Sulphur S8

Let's Define!

  • Atomicity: The total number of atoms present in a molecule.
  • Molecule: A particle formed when two or more atoms chemically bond together.
  1. Molecule of elements: Formed when two or more atoms of the same element chemically bond.
    Example: H2, P4, S8
  2. Molecule of compounds: Formed when two or more atoms of different elements chemically bond.
    Example: H2O, NH3, NaCl

Molecular Classification Details

Classification Core Rule Atomic Structure Common Examples
Molecule of Element Only identical chemical atoms bonded together. Hydrogen (H2), Oxygen (O2), Phosphorus (P4)
Molecule of Compound Two or more completely distinct chemical elements bonded. Water (H2O), Ammonia (NH3), Salt (NaCl)
