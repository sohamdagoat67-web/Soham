🧊 Solid Details

  • Tightly packed atomic particles.
  • Fixed geometric crystalline lattices.
  • Vibrates gently in place.
  • Definite shape and volume.

💧 Liquid Details

  • Close but fluidly arranged particles.
  • Slides easily past adjacent elements.
  • Adapts fluidly to container bounds.
  • Definite volume but no fixed shape.

💨 Gas Details

  • Widely separated chaotic atoms.
  • High-speed independent scattering motion.
  • Expands out to fill all available space.
  • Has neither fixed shape nor fixed volume.

⚡ Plasma Details

  • Superheated, highly ionized gas phase.
  • Electrons are stripped directly from cores.
  • Conducts electrical currents easily.
  • Responds powerfully to magnetic fields.

⚛️ B.E.C. Details

  • Supercooled near absolute zero (-273.15°C).
  • Atoms clump together into one macro-atom.
  • Behaves collectively like quantum waves.
  • Can drastically slow down traveling light beams.
