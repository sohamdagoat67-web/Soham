METALS & NON-METALS

Properties, uses and exceptions of elements with interactive revision quiz.

● Metals

Properties

Lustrous — shiny surfaceMalleable & Ductile — can be made into sheets & wiresGood Conductors — heat and electricitySonorous — produces ringing sound
Exceptions: Sodium is soft, Mercury is liquid at room temperature

● Non-Metals

Properties

Non-lustrous — dull appearanceBrittle — breaks easily when solidPoor Conductors — heat and electricityNon-sonorous — does not ring
Exceptions: Graphite conducts electricity, Bromine is liquid, Iodine is lustrous

● Uses of Metals

Fe
Iron
Bridges, nails, machines. Found in blood as haemoglobin.
Al
Aluminium
Foil, utensils. Alloy used for aeroplanes — lightweight & strong.
Cu
Copper
Electrical wires, TVs and computers.
Au
Gold
Ornaments and jewellery — noble metal.
Hg
Mercury
Thermometers — expands on heating.

● Uses of Non-Metals & Noble Gases

N
Nitrogen
Preserves food, used in fertilizers.
C
Carbon
Diamond for jewellery, graphite for pencils.
I
Iodine
Antiseptic, thyroid gland function.
He
Helium
Hot air balloons and welding.
Ar
Argon
Electric bulbs and tube lights.

● Revision Quiz

Test Your Knowledge

1. Which metal is the best conductor of electricity?
A) Iron (Fe)
B) Silver (Ag)
C) Aluminium (Al)
D) Copper (Cu)
Correct is Silver. It is not used for wires because it is expensive, so Copper is used.
2. Why is aluminium alloy used for aeroplanes?
A) Lightweight but very strong
B) Liquid at room temp
C) Poor conductor
D) Brittle
Aluminium alloy is lightweight and durable.
3. Where is iron found in our body?
A) In bones
B) Inside red blood cells as haemoglobin
C) In brain
D) In skin
Iron is found in red blood cells as haemoglobin.
4. Which is an exception among non-metals?
A) Graphite conducts electricity
B) Oxygen is a gas
C) Sulphur is brittle
D) Nitrogen preserves food
Graphite is a non-metal but a good conductor of electricity.
