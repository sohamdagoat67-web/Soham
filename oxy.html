🌍 What is Oxygen?

Oxygen is a chemical element essential for the respiration of most living organisms. It is represented by the symbol O and has atomic number 8.

Symbol: O
Atomic number: 8
Molecular formula of oxygen gas: O₂
State at room temperature: Gas
Discovery: Oxygen was independently discovered by Carl Wilhelm Scheele and Joseph Priestley in the 18th century.

🔬 Properties of Oxygen

  • It is colourless, odourless and tasteless.
  • It supports combustion but does not itself burn.
  • It is slightly soluble in water.
  • It combines with many elements to form oxides.
  • It makes up about 21% of Earth's atmosphere by volume.

🧪 Catalytic Decomposition of Hydrogen Peroxide

Hydrogen peroxide breaks down into water and oxygen. Manganese dioxide can act as a catalyst, speeding up the reaction without being used up overall.

2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂

Observation: Bubbles of oxygen gas are produced.

Type of reaction: Decomposition reaction.

Role of catalyst: It increases the reaction rate.

🌱 Importance of Oxygen

  • Essential for aerobic respiration.
  • Used in hospitals to support patients who need extra oxygen.
  • Supports combustion in many processes.
  • Used in steelmaking and certain industrial processes.
  • Liquid oxygen is used as an oxidiser in some rocket systems.
